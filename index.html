I'm a second-year Computer Science student at Stevens Institute of Technology (Quantitative Finance minor) who likes trying everything. I fell in love with CS because it's the closest thing to a superpower: if there's something I want to do, I can just do it, whether that's a hardware project, a piece of software, a tool, or something else.

When I find a challenge, I'll do anything to solve it, even if that means staying up until 4am. I'm passionate about finding solutions, and I do it purely for the love of the game.

A live simulation of how you break up a huge drone swarm. Instead of chasing every aircraft, it finds a small set of hub drones and cuts those links, splitting the swarm into pieces that can't talk to each other.
